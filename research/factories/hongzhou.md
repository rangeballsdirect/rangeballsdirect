# Hongzhou Golf Ball Factory

## Company

- Trading/company name: Shenzhen Hongzhou Sports Goods Co., Ltd.
- Current manufacturing company: Huizhou Hongzhou Sporting Goods Co., Ltd.
- Business: Golf-ball OEM/ODM manufacturing
- Established: 2006 (originally Shenzhen Hengshengyuan Rubber Co., Ltd.)

## Locations

- Production base: Second floor, Factory Building, Zone B, Zhenxing Industrial Park, No. 58 Jiangjun Road, Weibu Village, Qiuchang Town/Street, Huiyang District, Huizhou, Guangdong, China.
- Shenzhen contact address: Room 301, Building 2, No. 1 Junxin Industrial Road, Lingwu Village, Junlong Community, Guanlan Street, Longhua District, Shenzhen, Guangdong, China.

## Manufacturing profile

- Product types include one-layer practice/range balls, two-layer practice and match balls, three-layer and four-layer urethane balls, simulator balls, floating balls and custom/private-label balls.
- The company states that it operates a 10,000-square-metre production base and can deliver more than 10 million balls annually. Other pages on its website state 5,000 square metres, so floor-area claims require independent confirmation.
- The company states that it uses international-brand raw materials but does not publicly identify its rubber, ionomer or polyurethane suppliers.
- Own brand: supurNEWLING.
