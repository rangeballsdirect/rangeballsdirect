# Quality Control copy notes

These are working notes from the recent wording discussion, not approved website copy.

## Message to communicate

- Range Balls Direct is detail-driven. We visit factories, ask questions and understand what we are buying rather than choosing a range ball solely by its price.
- Core chemistry, material grades, cover materials and finish affect cost, flight, feel and durability. A cheaper specification is a deliberate choice, not automatically the right choice for every range.
- Different factories suit different specifications. We can choose from up to seven and usually work with two or three, selecting according to what a range needs.
- The approach is shaped by 30 years in professional golf and coaching, alongside a background in procurement. Do not imply the procurement background also spans 30 years or turn the page into a biography.
- Ask factories how they manage quality and what checks they perform. Do not imply that every factory holds the same formal quality-management certification.
- Ball-tracking ranges may value more consistent flight and performance. A well-made standard range ball can also be the right quality choice for a range without that technology. Do not claim that a higher-quality ball improves the radar equipment itself.
- Do not state that 90% of a ball's cost is in the core unless independently confirmed. Avoid unsupported claims about other suppliers.

## Recent wording drafts for reference

### Detail-driven buying

> We know how a range ball gets cheaper. Changing the core compounds, cover materials or finish can reduce cost, but each choice may affect flight, feel or durability. We visit factories and discuss those details rather than simply buying a ball at a price. That helps us choose the right specification for your range and explain what you’re getting.

### Different ranges, different needs

> As more ranges use ball-tracking technology, consistent flight and performance become especially important. But not every range needs a higher-spec ball or uses radar. A well-made standard range ball can be exactly the right choice. We look at how your range operates, then help choose the quality and specification that fits.
